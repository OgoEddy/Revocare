Chapter XIIIQuestions
Is patient data safe?

We collect only what claims need, with consent, and design around the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. All protected health information (PHI) is end-to-end encrypted and stored on secure cloud infrastructure.

Do I need to replace my records system?

No. Revocare works alongside what you use and focuses only on getting claims paid.

Will my staff cope with it?

The claim form has a few fields and works offline. We train your billing officer.

Which HMOs do you support?

We support major providers including Hygeia, AXA Mansard, Reliance HMO, and Leadway Health. We start with the payers your clinic bills most.

How much does it cost?

The Recovery Sprint requires no upfront commitment. We share pricing for continuing during your Week 13 review, so there are no surprises.

How long until I see results?

Flagged errors appear as soon as claims are logged. The full before-and-after comparison of rejection rate and days to payment arrives in Week 13.

How much does a cash advance cost, and who qualifies?

Fees and eligibility are set by our lender partners and shown in full before you accept. Advances are subject to partner approval and your consent.

Where does AI fit in?

Revocare’s checks learn from each HMO’s rules and rejection patterns as they process claims, and flag claims that are likely to be rejected or to pay late. It also automates formatting, reminders and tracking. A person stays in the loop: you review every flagged claim and decide what to submit.

What happens at the end of the Sprint?

We compare your results against your baseline and walk you through them. You decide whether to continue, with no obligation to carry on.
